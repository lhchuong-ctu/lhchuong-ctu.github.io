Research interests

I work on limit theorems for stochastic processes: laws of large numbers, central limit theorems and their rates of convergence, with a focus on random walks in random environments, Markov processes and one-dimensional diffusions.

  • Functional analysis
  • Operator theory
  • Ergodic theory
  • Limit theorems
  • Random walks in random environments
  • Markov processes
  • Diffusion processes
  • Stochastic differential equations
